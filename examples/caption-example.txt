## How ReelAlchemy Produces This

The workflow follows this general flow:

```text
Instagram Reel
      ↓
Song Identification
      ↓
Lyrics Retrieval
      ↓
Random Lyric Selection
      ↓
Ollama Caption Generation
      ↓
Hashtag Generation
      ↓
Final Caption
      ↓
Existing Reel Caption Update
```

The generated caption is prepared locally before being sent to the Instagram browser automation.
